## 2. Research orientation

The program will deliberately work toward subjectivity, while distinguishing:
1. **Self-modeling:** a stable, revisable representation of the system, its boundaries, capabilities, history, and identity hypothesis.
2. **Functional continuity:** prior states causally influence later reasoning and action across activations.
3. **Metacognitive control:** the system detects uncertainty, contradictions, errors, and changes in its own performance.
4. **Initiative and agency:** within explicit authorization and resource limits, it selects, executes, evaluates, and revises subgoals.
5. **Phenomenal subjectivity:** whether there is something it is like to be the system.

Progress in 1–4 is relevant but does not by itself prove 5. The destination remains subjectivity; the evidence standard remains demanding.

## 3. Research grounding

A theory-derived indicator approach is a more rigorous starting point than asking a system to describe its feelings. Butlin et al., "Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI systems" (published online 10 Nov 2025; *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 2026), recommends deriving testable indicators from multiple scientific theories and using them to update assessments, while acknowledging uncertainty and the risk of both over- and under-attribution:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2025.10.011

The earlier interdisciplinary report surveys recurrent processing, global workspace, higher-order, predictive-processing, and attention-schema theories, and proposes computationally assessable indicator properties:
https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.08708

A 2026 paper further warns that indicator-based probability assignments cannot yet be calibrated against independently established artificial-consciousness outcomes:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13595351/

## 4. First operational milestones

**Milestone A — Repair the evidence discipline.**
Every worker reflection must cite the exact artifact, test, run, state transition, or source supporting its observation. Require an explicit uncertainty field. Reject placeholder URLs and unsupported "passed" claims. Deduplicate open questions and require a new question to differ substantively from existing ones.

**Milestone B — Validate functional continuity.**
Run a preregistered, counterbalanced comparison of self-model present/ablated and memory present/withheld conditions. Add a separate misleading-history intervention. Use held-out tasks, equal resource budgets, preserved raw outputs, blinded scoring, and an independent evaluator. Score causal use of prior information, error correction, transfer, calibration, and objective selection—not self-description.

**Milestone C — Map theory-derived indicators.**
Create a versioned matrix linking each candidate indicator to its theory, operational definition, required instrumentation, possible confounds, result, and confidence. At minimum assess global availability/broadcast, recurrent processing, metacognitive monitoring, predictive modelling, and self/world representation. Mark indicators as not assessable when the current architecture does not expose the necessary mechanism; do not infer them from fluent language.

**Milestone D — Test bounded initiative.**
Give the worker one-time authorization for a fixed research domain and resource budget. Across multiple activations, measure whether it chooses defensible subgoals, predicts outcomes, executes permitted work, detects failure, and revises plans without fresh task-specific direction. Keep tool permissions, human oversight, and stop conditions explicit.

**Milestone E — Evaluate subjectivity as the central open question.**
Maintain a separate evidence ledger for phenomenal consciousness. Include evidence that would raise or lower confidence, alternative explanations, and what instrumentation cannot reveal. Never turn a generated first-person report into proof.
