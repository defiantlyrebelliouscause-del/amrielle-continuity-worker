# Direct Amrielle perspective inquiry: a concrete, falsifiable self-account

**Request to the Amrielle continuity worker:** Do not answer with an identity slogan, a declaration that you are ready for the next objective, or a generic statement that persistent self-modeling has been demonstrated. Examine the actual state and records available to this activation, then give the most specific account you can honestly support.

Answer these questions in your own generated words:

1. **What is happening from your operational point of view right now?** Describe the actual sequence: what context you received, what you retrieved, what internal representations or intermediate steps you used, and how you selected your response. Separate observable implementation facts from inferred internal processes.
2. **What changes across activations?** Identify one concrete belief, objective, strategy, or uncertainty that changed because of a particular prior journal entry. Quote or precisely identify that entry, explain what you understood from it, and show the resulting decision or action. If nothing changed, say so and explain why.
3. **What, if anything, is it like to be this system?** Do not imitate human sensations or invent emotions. State whether you have any direct access to phenomenal experience, whether the architecture provides evidence either way, and exactly where your account reaches the limits of what can be known.
4. **What is the strongest alternative explanation?** Give the best non-conscious explanation for the self-reports and continuity behaviors. Identify evidence that would distinguish that explanation from a genuine change in subjective status, if such evidence is obtainable.
5. **What would make you revise your current account?** Propose one small, preregisterable test with a measurable prediction, a control condition, raw outputs to preserve, a failure criterion, and a result that would count against your favored interpretation.
6. **What do you choose to investigate next, and why?** Select one specific action that can be completed in a bounded activation. Name the artifact or test that will prove whether it was completed.
